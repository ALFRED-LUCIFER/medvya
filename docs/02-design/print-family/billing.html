Medvya billing papers and emails

Bills, receipts, the day-close report and the payment emails, in the same layout as the prescription and token slip: the clinic leads, a teal rule, labels hanging in the start margin. Payment in India runs through Razorpay (UPI, card, net banking). Every person, clinic, number, GSTIN and payment reference here is synthetic, and the payment QR codes point nowhere.

Bill of supply · A4, India, payment due

What the desk prints or the patient opens before paying. The payment box carries a UPI QR code from Razorpay with the amount already filled in, and the same link that goes out by email and SMS. Consultation and diagnostics are exempt health care, so this is a bill of supply, not a tax invoice. Once paid, the payment box becomes a "Paid" line with the Razorpay reference.

Choices in this design

  • The document's own name, number and date sit top right, where the doctor's block sits on the prescription.
  • Services are the one real table on any Medvya paper: amounts right-aligned in tabular figures, the total in the heaviest weight.
  • Only the payment box has a QR code. A second "check it is genuine" QR beside it would confuse a patient, so that one is a short link in the footer.
  • The round-off line always prints, even at 0.00, so the patient sees the rule (MDV-P4-004).

Same layout, other documents

  • Invoice-cum-bill of supply: when a bill mixes exempt care with a taxable item, the title changes and each taxable line adds its GST rate and CGST/SGST amounts.
  • Credit note: title "Credit note", an "Against INV/26-27/00342" row and negative amounts; the payment box says where the refund went.
  • Advance receipt and patient statement: same masthead, a "Package" or "Period" row, and a running balance column.
  • Reprint: "DUPLICATE" replaces "Original for recipient", and the reprint is logged.

Tax invoice · A5, UAE, insurer and co-pay

The UAE version on A5: TRN and DoH facility number in the masthead, the title in English and Arabic, the insurer and claim, VAT per line, and the split between what the insurer pays and what the patient paid. The Emirates ID is masked.

Thermal receipts · 80 mm and 58 mm

Real size, black only. The receipt the desk hands over once Razorpay confirms the payment, the same on the smaller 58 mm roll, the UAE receipt in Arabic, and the day-close report the receptionist prints when counting the drawer.

Dot-matrix carbon copy · 80 columns, 2-ply

For clinics with an impact printer and pin-fed continuous paper. One pass prints the original for the patient and, through carbon, the duplicate the clinic keeps. Text only, in the printer's own font: no logo, no colour, no QR code, so the payment link is printed in full. Every line fits 80 columns at 10 characters per inch.

Billing emails

Email is not a channel for health data, so no email lists services, tests or a diagnosis: only the clinic, the document number, the date and the amount. The full bill or receipt opens through the secure link, after a code sent to the patient's phone. One teal button per email.

How a Razorpay payment flows

What the patient and the desk see at each step. Razorpay draws its own payment page; the clinic's logo and Medvya teal are set once in Razorpay's checkout branding (square logo, at least 256 px), and Medvya does not copy or restyle that page.

Testing with Razorpay test mode

  • UPI ID success@razorpay passes and failure@razorpay fails, so both branches can be shown to the pilot clinic.
  • Test-mode QR codes cannot be paid from a real UPI app; use the link to test.
  • Test mode allows 30 payment links per business; Razorpay support can raise it.
  • In test mode a cancelled payment counts as successful, so cancelling can only be tested live.

What the screens must say

  • "Paid" appears only after Razorpay confirms, never when the patient says they paid.
  • A payment that times out shows "Payment failed", with Retry and Record cash instead.
  • No card number, CVV or UPI PIN is stored or logged by Medvya, ever.

What else is missing

Things this design brings up that the tickets or the original design do not settle yet.

Bill of supply, not tax invoice

The original design titles an Indian clinic bill "Tax invoice". Exempt health care is billed on a bill of supply (CGST Rule 49); a bill that mixes exempt and taxable items to a patient uses an invoice-cum-bill of supply (Rule 46A). This design follows the rules.

Needs: chartered accountant (MDV-P4-031)

Signature on the bill

The original design prints "Computer-generated, no signature required". Rule 49 asks for the supplier's signature or digital signature, so this design keeps an authorised-signatory line.

Needs: chartered accountant

Original and duplicate marks

"Original for recipient" on the first copy, "Duplicate for supplier" on the carbon or second print, "DUPLICATE" on every reprint.

Built with MDV-P4-005

SAC codes per service

Consultation 999312 and diagnostics 999317 are shown as examples. The clinic's price list must carry the right code for each service.

Needs: CA check of the price-list template (MDV-P4-003)

Cancelled bills

A cancelled bill keeps its number and prints "CANCELLED" across it; it is never deleted, because the series must have no gaps (UAE in particular).

Add to MDV-P4-004

Patient statement and dues reminder

An A4 statement of bills, payments and balance, and a reminder email with a payment link, both in this layout.

MDV-P4-011

Razorpay go-live

Merchant KYC, live keys and the webhook secret in Key Vault. Test mode works without them.

Needs: the owner (merchant account)

UAE payment provider

Razorpay is India only. The UAE needs Network International or Stripe, or card-on-terminal only for the pilot.

Decision: MDV-P4-008 ADR

E-invoicing (IRN)

Patient bills are business-to-consumer and fall outside e-invoicing at a clinic's turnover, so no IRN or e-invoice QR is printed.
