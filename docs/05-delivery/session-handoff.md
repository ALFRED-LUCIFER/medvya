Until then the list is a placeholder, and the Settings screen says so.
- **Consultation and chart upgrade (epic MDV-P2-E07, 12 Nov 2026 design):** a clinician signs off, before the pilot uses them: the growth reference tables and centile flags (WHO 0–5 y; IAP or UAE charts for older children, MDV-P2-035), the immunisation schedules (the design's IAP 2023 reading is marked "verify"; UAE schedule, MDV-P2-036), the pregnancy dating rule and antenatal contact plan (MDV-P2-034), the older-adult flag lines and medicine-review rules (MDV-P2-037), the care-gap rules, their intervals (a year for eye, foot and urine albumin; 14 days before an unresulted test is a gap; the OGTT window 24–28 weeks) and the default chronic care targets (HbA1c < 7 %, BP < 130/80, LDL < 100 mg/dL, eGFR ≥ 60, UACR < 30 mg/g; `CareTargets`, `CareGapRules`, MDV-P2-038), the new vitals and lab reference ranges (MDV-P2-031), and every pregnancy, child-dose, kidney and older-adult prescribing rule (MDV-P2-040). Each rule set ships with `SignOff` empty and says so on screen. Also the review-of-systems symptoms and examination systems of each template version and the template suggestion (pregnancy problem, under 18, from 65, active E10–E14 diabetes; `NoteTemplates`, MDV-P2-042), taken from the design; the doctor can always change the template.
- **Prescription safety rules (MDV-P2-016):** the allergy classes, interactions and adult maximum daily doses in `SafetyRules` (`src/Modules/Prescribing/Medvya.Prescribing.Domain/SafetyChecks.cs`, version `2026.11-draft-5`) are a small curated set, not a licensed drug-interaction database. A clinical safety reviewer must sign the set off before the R2 exit (MDV-P2-028), and someone chooses and licenses an interaction knowledge base to replace it. The screen says so in its "About these checks" panel. The patient rules added by MDV-P2-040 (NSAIDs from 20 weeks of pregnancy, paracetamol and ibuprofen by weight for a child, and with no recent weight a severe finding plus a per-dose ceiling by age band (`ChildAgeCeilings`, #1085), NSAID and metformin eGFR thresholds, a Beers subset from 65) are in the same sign-off. The latest eGFR on the chart (MDV-P2-031) feeds the kidney rules at any age; an open pregnancy (MDV-P2-034) feeds the weeks, and a woman 12-55 with neither an open pregnancy nor a "not pregnant" statement made today is reported as not checked.
- **Organisation onboarding and approval (ADR-016, owner decision 10 Nov 2026; replaces "Medvya verifies each doctor's licence", MDV-P1-007 default and MDV-P2-023):** Medvya approves the organisation (Clinic or Hospital), the owner attests each doctor's licence. Needs a person: counsel confirms the per-type document lists, the Terms and DPA wording on the owner's liability (P1-044, P1-067, P2-022); a named Medvya operations reviewer and an SLA for approvals (P2-023). Decided 10 Nov: set-up-only mode before approval, a hard-coded starter plan of 1 doctor and 2 staff (P1-069), documents per type, owner-attested licence, controlled-drug risk accepted. Open: whether an expired attested licence blocks signing (default warn only). The gate and the owner attestation are built (P1-063, P1-065), and so are the onboarding request and the interim approval API (P1-062, P1-064, runbook `docs/runbooks/organisation-approval.md`): set `Operations:OrganisationApprovers` (and `OrganisationReaders`) on Azure to the Medvya operator user ids, and give each operator a Medvya account. New clinics wait at Draft or Submitted until an operator approves them; the console queue page is not built yet.
- **UX audit clinical items (#1121, #1122, 12 Nov 2026):** a clinician decides (1) the age band in which the chart shows the pregnancy card in full: built as 15 to 49 (the WHO reproductive-age definition), folded under its heading from 10 to 14 and 50 to 55 (`pregnancyOffer` in `web/clinic/src/features/patient360/domain/pregnancy.ts`); (2) whether nurse intake takes temperature in °F (conversion, rounding and the plausibility limits in °F), and (3) whether intake records random blood sugar, with its unit (mg/dL in India, mmol/L in the UAE), plausibility limits and flag thresholds. (2) and (3) are not built until then.
- **Prescription print format (MDV-AUDIT-1097, #1097):** a clinical or regulatory advisor reviews the printed prescription against the NMC Registered Medical Practitioner (Professional Conduct) Regulations 2002 format (RMP name, qualifications and registration number, clinic, patient name, age and sex, date, diagnosis, medicines by generic name, advice, signature) and the Hindi, Tamil and Arabic wording. Built from stored data, in the print family layout (MDV-P1-111): the masthead (logo, clinic and location, address, phone and, from #1365 F-068, the registration number as entered in Settings), doctor qualifications and licence, patient age, the date and time of authorisation, diagnosis, advice, follow-up date, the QR check with the content hash, and the signature box: the signature image when the doctor uploaded one, otherwise a blank space above the line for a signature by hand. Not stored, so not printed: the patient's address and the doctor's qualification where a profile has none. Until a named person signs the layout off, each doctor still approves it as their own (layout version 5; an approval of an earlier version does not carry over).
- **UX validation sessions and people items (MDV-P1-108, epic MDV-P1-E12):** the pilot doctor, nurse, receptionist and owner sessions and the 21 decisions of `docs/product-ux-audit/11-pilot-doctor-validation-plan.md` (rows 1 to 5 before the first safety ticket merges), the doctor's list of about 100 medicines with brands and a named reviewer for the class mapping (MDV-P1-088), the staff training date and whether screens may change shape before it, the on-site device check (mouse or touch; one run on a real tablet), and the vitals ranges sign-off (#956) before MDV-P2-048. ## 9 · Known limits and shortcuts (each is written where it lives)

Audit window is per-instance memory (`AuditWindow`); move to the database before a second API instance. Hangfire runs inside `Host.Api` and jobs are off in Azure. No private endpoints, no Defender, no staging slot; SQL reachable via the app's outbound addresses. Long-term backup retention off in dev. Guest-account MFA lives in the personal Microsoft account; production moves staff sign-in to a dedicated Entra External ID tenant. Consent wording is DRAFT. The 5-minute grid rule is written in both `OnboardingRules` and `ScheduleRules` (a test keeps them in step). Free tier: restore drill creates a billed serverless database for a short time.

## 10 · Working rules and gotchas that bit us

- **Shell:** `dotnet` is in `~/.dotnet`: `export PATH="$HOME/.dotnet:$PATH"`. Docker: `DOCKER_HOST=unix://$HOME/.colima/default/docker.sock TESTCONTAINERS_DOCKER_SOCKET_OVERRIDE=/var/run/docker.sock`. Several agents in parallel: `MSBUILDDISABLENODEREUSE=1 DOTNET_CLI_USE_MSBUILD_SERVER=0` and `-- xUnit.ParallelizeTestCollections=false`. zsh does not expand `--include=*.cs` globs (use `bash -c`); macOS `sed -i ''`. A `!(command)` in the prompt does not run: use `! command`. `gh workflow run deploy-dev.yml --ref main` with nothing after `main`.
- **Process:** never merge with red CI; never `--admin`; never weaken a test or guard. Every safety test is mutation-checked (the change is removed and the test must fail). Warnings are errors. Synthetic data only (names end "(synthetic)", mails `@example.com`).
- **Git:** do not hand-edit `docs/05-delivery/tickets/_source/*.tickets` progress lines, Gherkin tags or generated `tickets/`; edit the source only to change scope or acceptance criteria. A shared checkout can be switched by another session: check `git branch --show-current` before committing.
- **Agents:** give each agent non-overlapping files; tell them not to edit `tickets/**`; ask for mutation checks and exact commands; they often skip the full suite (CI runs it) and manual browser checks. The Playwright MCP browser is shared: one agent at a time; call tools one at a time and never batch a screenshot with a navigation.
- **Live testing:** sessions expire after idle (designed): click "Sign in again", pick the clinic. `/app/` pages show "Loading…" for several seconds after a full load. The telemetry scrubber redacts logged values: one fixed message per reason, never `{Reason}` arguments.
- **Deploy check for the organisation migration (MDV-P1-068):** after the first deploy that carries it, sign in to the Azure dev tenant as its owner and book a visit: the tenant must be Approved and the doctors show "Attested by" with no new step. The integration tests cover the migration; only this run proves it on Azure.
- **Plan cap is a setting (MDV-P1-069, 15 Nov 2026):** `Plan__Doctors` and `Plan__Staff` (default 1 and 2; dev 3 and 5 from `infra/bicep/main.bicep`, live only after the next `infra-dev` apply); a wrong value stops the app at start-up.
- **Deploy side effects:** a deploy restarts the app (~2 min of 502). An infrastructure apply that changes app settings also restarts it.
- **Tool limits (6 Nov):** a tool call that starts with `sleep` is blocked: wait with a background `until` loop instead. Colima's socket died once under parallel test runs: `colima restart`, then `docker ps`; agents must not restart it themselves. e2e uses ports 4173 and 4174: a stale server from another session gives false results (check `lsof -iTCP:4173`). Every merge to `main` restarts CI and cancels the earlier run: tag only the commit whose runs finished green.
- **CI:** the `web` job runs lint, unit tests, the production build and the mocked-API e2e (more than 250 specs); it hit its old 10-minute limit, now 20. If it grows again, shard Playwright (`--shard`) instead of raising the cap.
