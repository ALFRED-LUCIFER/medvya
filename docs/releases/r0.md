## 3 · Known issues and shortcuts (not exhaustive: each is recorded where it lives)

- Deploys cause about a two-minute outage (free App Service plan has no staging slot) (#116).
- Background jobs are switched off in the deployed environment, so appointment reminders are not sent. Email invitations go through Azure Communication Services Email (data location India).
- The access-log audit window and some limits are per instance (see Section 2).
- Deployed environment has one hand-created test clinic, not the seeded synthetic tenant (#151).
- Hindi and Arabic translations of recent messages are unreviewed; consent wording is draft pending legal review (MDV-P1-044).
